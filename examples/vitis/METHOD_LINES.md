# Method-level lines: parentage, recombination, TE annotation, mitochondrial genomes

Written 2026-11-21 (UTC+8). Four lines of work were earlier described in this repository only as handled elsewhere. Each now has a page at method level, with public data only. The pages describe plans and public inputs; they hold no results and no private data.

| Line | Page | Status | What the page holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family parentage and relationship checks | [project 23](projects/23-family-parentage/STATUS.md) | Plan fixed | Design that keeps pedigree evidence, offspring, meioses and informative intervals apart; public route through DS10, DS12, DS02 and the DS18 trio |
| Pedigree and linkage-disequilibrium recombination maps | [project 24](projects/24-recombination-maps/STATUS.md) | Plan fixed | Design that keeps a recent pedigree quantity and a historical LD-based quantity apart; public route through published maps and public panels |
| TE annotation methods, library curation and automation | [project 25](projects/25-te-annotation-methods/STATUS.md) | Plan fixed | Outline of a methods-centred survey; grape assemblies and pangenomes as case studies; split with project 14 and the vitis-te catalogue |
| Mitochondrial genomes, NUMTs and heteroplasmy | [project 26](projects/26-mitochondrial-genomes/STATUS.md) | Not started | Proposed starting design, public organelle papers and long-read inputs; the defining question is still open |

## What is not in this repository

Some of this work was first designed on a private genotype panel with a private reference sequence and reported family relationships. The panel, the sample list, the relationships and any numbers from that work are not here, and the pages do not depend on them. Where a page needs something that is not public, it says [to confirm] instead of guessing.
