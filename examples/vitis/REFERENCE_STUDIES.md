# Reference studies and scope updates

Added 2026-11-26. For each project, one or more published studies that already did similar work in another species are listed on the project page, with species, what was done, the figures used and what a grape results section would need. Each project page also states the bar a grape paper must meet to compete for a first-quartile (Q1) journal. DOIs were checked on Crossref. Figures are described, not copied; any redraw is marked "Redrawn after" the source. Where only figure titles were read, or the full text was not read, the page says so.

## Projects whose scope changed on 2026-11-26

Each of these pages has a section "Scope update (2026-11-26)" after its Question. The aim for each is a paper that can compete for a first-quartile (Q1) journal in its field; each section lists what that requires and a fallback.

- [03](projects/03-endogenous-florendoviruses/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Endogenous florendovirus insertions across grape haplotypes: What grape adds beyond existing work.
- [05](projects/05-gene-pav-across-pangenomes/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Gene presence/absence across grape pangenomes under one pipeline: What grape adds beyond existing work.
- [06](projects/06-vitis-pan-nlrome/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) A genus-wide NLR catalogue with integrated domains and collapse checks: What grape adds beyond existing work.
- [11](projects/11-sequence-to-function-models/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Sequence-to-expression models and plant foundation models evaluated on grape: New scope: a methods benchmark only.
- [12](projects/12-convergent-domestication-cross-crop/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Convergent domestication: grape compared with other crops in one pipeline: Null result preregistered as publishable.
- [16](projects/16-graph-sv-load/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Inherited and clonal structural variants on a graph: where hemizygous SVs sit and why: Narrowed scope: inherited versus clonal hemizygous differences within grape.
- [19](projects/19-public-rnaseq-on-t2t/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Public RNA-seq on PN_T2T: expression atlas, eQTL/sQTL and allele-specific expression: What grape adds beyond existing work.
- [22](projects/22-sex-region-population-level/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Population-level evolution of the sex-determining region (demoted): Stays demoted; one question kept.
- [25](projects/25-te-annotation-methods/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Transposable-element annotation methods, library curation and automation: New scope: a grape case study with TE_Bench only.
- [26](projects/26-mitochondrial-genomes/README.md#scope-update-2026-11-26) Grapevine mitochondrial genomes, nuclear mitochondrial insertions and heteroplasmy: Option (c) is dropped.
