# Preregistration template

Fill this in for a project, commit it with a date, and only then look at results. Criteria from any earlier attempt at a project are reference only; every restart gets a new preregistration.

## 1. Project and version

- Project folder:
- Date and commit:
- Who wrote it, who reviewed it (the writer does not review):

## 2. Questions and hypotheses

One line per hypothesis, matching the rows in the project README.

## 3. Primary outcome

The one result the paper stands or falls on.

## 4. Data

- Accessions and the date of the file lists:
- Inclusion and exclusion rules (decided before seeing results):
- Sample identity checks (AM04) and how duplicates and clones are handled:

## 5. Analysis

- Modules, tool versions, parameters:
- Thresholds and tests for each hypothesis:
- One- or two-sided for each test, and why:
- Null model, and the permutation or resampling unit:
- Unit of analysis (sample, genotype, genotype × tissue, clone, accession, haplotype) and how pseudo-replication is handled (clones, relatives, two haplotypes of one accession, neighbouring windows):
- Power or minimum detectable effect, and the basis for the sample size; the minimum per group below which the analysis is descriptive only:
- α or effect-size threshold for each test; the primary result is reported regardless of its direction:
- Multiple-testing control (family and method):
- Missing data and exclusions: how missing genotypes, phenotypes, or metadata are handled, and that any exclusion made after seeing data is reported as a deviation:
- Sensitivity analyses (list):
- Post-hoc analyses: allowed, but labelled post hoc in every table and figure and never used as the primary outcome.

## 6. Stop rules

What ends the project or turns it into a descriptive or methods report.

## 7. Deviations

Any change after this date is logged here. Deviations do not overwrite the original text.
